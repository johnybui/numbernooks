Number Nooks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Number Nooks is a math game for young children (ages 5 to 8), available for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: Number Nooks does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

At a glance
  • No accounts, sign-ins or names.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No internet connection is used by the game.
  • Game progress stays on the device.

Information we collect

None. Number Nooks does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. The game does not ask for any device permissions (such as camera, microphone, location or contacts).

Information stored on the device

To let a child continue where they left off, the game saves a small file on the device itself. It contains only:

  • which worlds and levels have been reached and the stars earned;
  • game settings, such as music on or off, holiday themes on or off, and the chosen costumes.

This file never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups), which are governed by Apple's or Google's policies.

Children's privacy

Number Nooks is designed for children. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is no personal information for parents to review or delete. We designed the app to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Apple App Store Kids category guidelines and the Google Play Families Policy. The app contains no advertising, no links out of the app and no social features.

Third parties

The game includes no third-party services, advertising networks or analytics tools, so no information is shared with third parties. Apple and Google run the stores where you download the app and may collect information about downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Security

Because the game handles no personal information and does not connect to the internet, there is no personal data to transmit or protect on servers.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a future version needed to collect any personal information from children, we would first obtain verifiable parental consent as required by law.
