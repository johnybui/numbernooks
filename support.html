Number Nooks Support

Help for parents and teachers

Number Nooks is a math adventure for kids in grades 1 to 6, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Children pick their grade, then help the Numberlings bring the colors back to the Rainbow Tree by solving math problems that follow the US Common Core standards.

Need help or have an idea?

Email johnybui@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8) and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I change the grade?

On the map, tap GRADE · CHANGE at the top, then pick a grade. Each grade keeps its own progress, so switching back never loses stars.

Is progress saved?

Yes. Stars, unlocked levels and settings are saved automatically on the device. Progress is not shared between devices, because the game has no accounts and never connects to the internet.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again. This clears all progress on that device.

Does it need the internet? Are there ads or purchases?

No. The game works fully offline, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Something isn't working

  • Close the app completely and open it again.
  • Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Restart the device.
  • Still stuck? Email us with your device and what you see.

Privacy

Number Nooks collects no personal information. Read the full privacy policy.
